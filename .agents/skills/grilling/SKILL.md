# Grilling

Interview the user to reach shared understanding of a plan or design.

## Rules

1. Ask one question at a time. Wait for the answer before the next one.
2. Walk dependencies in order: a decision that unlocks others comes first.
3. For each question, state your recommended answer and why.
4. If the codebase, docs, or `TODO.md` can answer a question, read them and do not ask.
5. Challenge vague terms; ask for the exact meaning and check it against `GLOSSARY.md`.
6. Keep going until every branch is resolved or explicitly deferred.
7. End with a short summary of decisions, open items, and where each decision is recorded.
